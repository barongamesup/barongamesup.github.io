Privacy Policy for Soil First

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Soil First (“the app”) is a care journal for your houseplants — it plans watering and other care around a quick soil check, keeps a history of what you did, lets you note how each plant is doing, and follows its growth in photos. It is published by Baron Mobile Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Soil First Android application distributed through Google Play.

The short version

Everything you record stays on your phone. There is no account to create, no server to sync to, and no way for us to see your plants, your notes or your photos. The app works offline; the only thing it uses the internet for is showing advertisements, and our advertising provider collects some device data to deliver them. That is the only data collection involved, and it never includes anything you record in the app.

This data is written to your device’s private app storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to read it. There is no account, no login and no cloud sync — apart from loading advertisements, the app works completely offline. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently, unless you have exported a backup yourself (see below).

The app opts out of Android’s automatic device backup, so Android does not copy its data — or your photos — to your Google account either.

Seasons, without your location

Care intervals stretch in winter and shorten in summer. To know which season it is where you are, the app guesses your hemisphere from your phone’s region setting (for example English (United Kingdom)); you can change it in Settings. The app never asks for your location, has no location permission, and does not use weather services.

Photos you add

You can add a growth photo, a cover photo or a photo to a check-in in two ways. Choose from library opens Android’s photo picker: only the pictures you choose are handed to the app, and nothing else in your photo library is read. Take photo opens the camera; the app asks for camera permission at that moment, the first time you tap it, and not before. If you refuse, you can still add photos from your library.

Each photo you add is re-saved as a new copy in the app’s private storage, together with a thumbnail. The copies stay on your phone, are never uploaded, and are not part of a backup file. They are deleted when you remove the photo, when you clear archived plants’ photos in Settings → Your data, when you delete a plant for good, or when you uninstall the app. Your original pictures are left untouched wherever they were.

Backups and restore

Your data leaves the app only when you send it somewhere yourself, from Settings → Your data → Backup:

  • Export backup puts your plants, rooms, care tasks and history, check-ins, progress and settings — never your photos — into a single file and opens Android’s share sheet, so you choose where it goes: your own Google Drive, an email, a folder. The file goes only where you send it, and that destination’s own privacy practices apply to it. We never receive a copy.
  • Restore from a backup reads a backup file you pick with Android’s file picker. The app shows what the file contains and asks before it adds anything; a restore adds what is missing and keeps the newer copy where both have the same item, and never deletes anything already on the phone. On a new phone you can also start a restore from the welcome screen.

The file created for sharing is kept in the app’s private storage until your next export, until Android clears it, or until you uninstall the app.

Notifications

The app can send you one daily digest at the time you choose (9:00 unless you change it), and only on days when some of your plants need care. It counts plants — “3 plants need attention today” — and never names them. You can also turn on a streak safety net, off unless you enable it: one evening reminder on a day your care streak is still at risk. Nothing is sent during your quiet hours.

On Android 13 and later your phone asks for permission to show notifications right after you add your first plant, or when you tap Allow notifications in Settings → Notifications — not when you install the app. If you refuse, the app works the same; its Today list still shows what needs care.

Notifications are scheduled locally on your phone. They are not push messages: nothing is sent from a server, and no one is told that a notification fired or what you did with it. You can pause every notification for a few days or weeks, or turn them off, in Settings → Notifications.

Progress, badges and streak freezes

The app keeps a light record of your care — care you logged, check-ins, growth photos — to award points, levels, a streak and badges. Points are never tied to how often you water. This record is stored with the rest of your data on your phone and is not sent anywhere. You can earn an extra streak freeze by watching a rewarded advertisement, described below; it never grants points, levels or badges, nothing else in the app is tied to advertisements, and no feature is locked behind one.

Deleting your data

You can remove photos, care tasks and rooms you no longer use, and delete an archived plant for good, which removes its photos from your phone. The plant’s past care entries stay in the app’s own database — they still count towards your streak and badges — until you clear the app’s storage. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device settings (Settings → Apps → Soil First → Storage → Clear storage), removes everything, including photos and any backup file still waiting in the app’s cache.

Because we keep nothing anywhere else, that is a complete deletion — there is no copy on a server for us to remove, and nothing for you to request from us. Backups you exported yourself are in the places you put them.

Advertising

Soil First is free and is funded by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display:

  • a banner at the bottom of My Plants, Insights and a plant’s own screen;
  • an occasional full-screen advertisement right after you add a plant and return to your collection — at most once each time you open the app. Never when the app opens, and never in the middle of a task;
  • a rewarded video advertisement, only when you choose Watch an ad — earn a streak freeze on your Profile. You are never shown one otherwise.

The first time you open the app it shows no advertisements at all. Advertisements are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences. Nothing you record in the app is ever sent to our advertising provider or used to select an advertisement; the part of the app that requests advertisements has no access to your plants, notes or photos.

Your choices about advertising

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform. It asks whether Google and its advertising partners may store and access information on your device, such as the advertising ID, and use it for personalised advertising. You may refuse. If you refuse, you will still see advertisements, but they will not be personalised, and the app works exactly the same either way.

You can change your decision at any time from Settings → Your data → Ad privacy options. That option appears only where the consent form applies.

Elsewhere, advertisements may be personalised using the advertising ID. Everywhere in the world you can limit this through your device: Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads), where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.

Analytics and crash reporting

There are none. The app contains no analytics library and no crash reporter, so we receive no usage statistics, no session data and no crash reports containing your information. What we can see is limited to the anonymous, aggregated figures Google Play and AdMob show every developer.

Children

Soil First is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase on request. To exercise rights relating to data collected for advertising — including access, erasure, or objection under the GDPR — please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy, which describes how to make such a request. You can delete all locally stored data at any time as described under Deleting your data above.
