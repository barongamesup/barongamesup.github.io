Privacy Policy for Skillforge

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Skillforge (“the app”) is a personal-development app published by Baron Mobile Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Skillforge Android application distributed through Google Play.

The short version

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect or receive any personal information about you ourselves. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to export it yourself. The app does show advertisements, and our advertising provider — Google AdMob — collects data to deliver them. That is the only data collection involved.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the display name you choose during setup, the categories, skills and tasks you create, which tasks you have completed, and your XP total locally on your device.

This data is written to your device’s private app storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, it is not backed up to any server we control, and we have no way to read it. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Backing up your own data

Settings → Back up my data writes everything the app has stored into a single file and hands it to your device’s share sheet. What happens to that file next is entirely your choice — save it to your phone, send it to yourself, or put it in a cloud drive. It is not sent to us and we never receive a copy. Once you share it with another app or service, that file is covered by their privacy policy rather than this one.

Settings → Restore from a backup reads a file you pick and replaces the data on this device with it. Nothing is uploaded.

Advertising

Skillforge is free and is funded by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display a banner on the main screen and an occasional full-screen advertisement after a task is completed.

To serve and measure these advertisements, Google may collect and process information such as:

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We only receive anonymous, aggregated reporting such as the total number of advertisements shown. Google’s handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use its partners’ apps

Your choices about advertising

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You choose whether your data may be used for personalised advertising. You may refuse. If you refuse, you will still see advertisements, but they will not be personalised.

You can change your decision at any time by opening the app and tapping “Privacy Settings” on the main screen.

Everywhere in the world, you can limit advertising tracking through your device: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, photos or files.

Children

Skillforge is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase on request. To exercise rights relating to data collected by Google for advertising — including access, erasure, or objection under the GDPR — please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy, which describes how to make such a request. You can also delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated date at the top. Significant changes affecting how data is handled will also be reflected in a new app release.
