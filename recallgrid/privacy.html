Privacy Policy for Recall Grid

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Recall Grid (“the app”) is a memory game published by Baron Mobile Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Recall Grid Android application distributed through Google Play.

The short version

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect or receive any personal information about you ourselves. Your game progress never leaves your phone. The app does show advertisements, and our advertising provider — Google AdMob — collects data to deliver them. That is the only data collection involved.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally so you can continue where you left off. This consists of the highest level you have unlocked, your best score for each level you finished, your current and best run of consecutive wins, the number of hints you hold, and your settings (sound, haptics, colourblind mode and practice mode).

This data is written to your device’s private app storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, it is not backed up to any server we control, and we have no way to read it. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently. You can also erase it from inside the app at any time with Settings → Reset Progress.

Advertising

Recall Grid is free and is funded by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display three kinds of advertisement:

  • a banner on the home and level-select screens;
  • an occasional full-screen advertisement between rounds;
  • optional rewarded advertisements, which you may choose to watch in exchange for an extra hint or for continuing a failed round. These are never forced — nothing in the game requires you to watch one.

No advertisement is shown during the first five levels, and none is shown while you are playing a round.

To serve and measure these advertisements, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • How Google uses data when you use its partners’ apps

Your choices about advertising

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You choose whether your data may be used for personalised advertising. You may refuse. If you refuse, you will still see advertisements, but they will not be personalised.

You can change your decision at any time by opening the app and tapping “Privacy Settings” in the app’s Settings screen.

Everywhere in the world, you can limit advertising tracking through your device: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.

Children

Recall Grid is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase on request. To exercise rights relating to data collected by Google for advertising — including access, erasure, or objection under the GDPR — please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy, which describes how to make such a request. You can also delete all locally stored game data at any time with Settings → Reset Progress, by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated date at the top. Significant changes affecting how data is handled will also be reflected in a new app release.
