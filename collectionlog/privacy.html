Privacy Policy for CollectionLog

Last updated: 26 September 2026

CollectionLog (“the app”) is a private catalogue for any kind of personal collection — records, comics, watches, building sets, cards, perfume, or anything you define yourself — published by Baron Mobile Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the CollectionLog Android application distributed through Google Play.

The short version

Everything you catalogue stays on your phone. There is no account to create, no server to sync to, and no way for us to see what you own. The app works offline; the only thing it uses the internet for is showing advertisements, and our advertising provider collects some device data to deliver them. That is the only data collection involved, and it never includes anything you record in the app.

This data is written to your device’s private app storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to read it. There is no account, no login and no cloud sync — apart from loading advertisements, the app works completely offline. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently.

One thing sits outside the app: if you have turned on Android’s own device backup, Android may include the app’s data in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so that your catalogue can be restored on a new phone. That backup is made and managed by Android and Google on your behalf; we cannot access it. You can turn device backup off in your phone’s backup settings.

Photos you add

You can add photos to an item in two ways. Choose from library opens Android’s photo picker: only the pictures you choose are handed to the app, and nothing else in your photo library is read. Take photo opens the camera; the app asks for camera permission at that moment, the first time you tap it, and not before. If you refuse, you can still add photos from your library.

Each photo you add is re-saved as a new copy in the app’s private storage, together with a thumbnail. The copies stay on your phone, are never uploaded, and are deleted when you remove the photo, delete the item, or uninstall the app. Your original pictures are left untouched wherever they were.

Backups, restore and erasing everything

Your data leaves the app only when you send it somewhere yourself, from Settings → Data:

  • Export backup packs your collections, items, sets, tags and photos into a single backup file and opens Android’s share sheet, so you choose where it goes — a file, an email, a cloud drive. The file goes only where you send it, and that destination’s own privacy practices apply to it. We never receive a copy.
  • Restore from backup reads a backup file you pick with Android’s file picker and replaces everything on the device with its contents. The app tells you what the file contains before it does so.
  • Delete all data erases every collection, item, photo, set and tag on the device, along with your progress.

Files created for sharing are kept in the app’s private storage until Android clears them or you uninstall the app.

Notifications

The app can send you one gentle reminder a day to add something to your archive. It is off until you turn it on in Settings → Reminders; on Android 13 and later your phone asks for permission to show notifications at that moment — not when you install the app. The reminder is skipped on days you have already catalogued something, and it is not sent at all on days when the app has nothing to nudge you about.

Notifications are scheduled locally on your phone. They are not push messages: nothing is sent from a server, and no one is told that a reminder fired or what you did with it. The notification text is the same every time and names nothing from your catalogue. Turning the reminder off in Settings → Reminders cancels it.

Progress, badges and unlockable themes

The app keeps a light record of your activity — items added, photos attached, sets completed — to award points, levels, a streak and badges. This record is stored with the rest of your data on your phone and is not sent anywhere. The optional accent themes can be unlocked by watching a rewarded advertisement, described below; nothing else in the app is tied to advertisements, and no feature is locked behind one.

Deleting your data

Every item, photo, tag, set and collection can be deleted individually, and Settings → Data → Delete all data erases everything at once. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device settings (Settings → Apps → CollectionLog → Storage → Clear data), removes everything, including photos and any backup file still in the cache.

Because we keep nothing anywhere else, that is a complete deletion — there is no copy on a server for us to remove, and nothing for you to request from us. Backups you exported yourself, and a device backup you have turned on in Android, are in the places you put them.

Advertising

CollectionLog is free and is funded by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display:

  • a banner at the bottom of a collection’s item list and of the Explore tab;
  • an occasional full-screen advertisement after you save an item — at least three minutes after the previous one, and only once you have done several things in the app since then. Never when the app opens, never in the middle of a task, and never next to a delete;
  • a rewarded video advertisement, only when you choose Watch an ad to unlock for an accent theme in Settings → Appearance. You are never shown one otherwise.

Nothing you record in the app is ever sent to our advertising provider or used to select an advertisement. The part of the app that requests advertisements has no access to your catalogue.

Your choices about advertising

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform. It asks whether Google and its advertising partners may store and access information on your device, such as the advertising ID, and use it for personalised advertising. You may refuse. If you refuse, you will still see advertisements, but they will not be personalised, and the app works exactly the same either way.

You can change your decision at any time from Settings → About → Ad privacy options. That option appears only where the consent form applies.

Everywhere in the world, you can limit advertising tracking through your device: Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads), where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.

Analytics and crash reporting

There are none. The app contains no analytics library and no crash reporter, so we receive no usage statistics, no session data and no crash reports containing your information. What we can see is limited to the anonymous, aggregated figures Google Play and AdMob show every developer.

Children

CollectionLog is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase on request. To exercise rights relating to data collected for advertising — including access, erasure, or objection under the GDPR — please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy, which describes how to make such a request. You can delete all locally stored data at any time as described under Deleting your data above.
